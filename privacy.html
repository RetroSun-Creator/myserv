Privacy Policy

MyServ · Effective 24 October 2026

MyServ is a vehicle maintenance log with a 3D view of your car. It is built so that your information stays on your iPhone. We do not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter — vehicles, maintenance records, notes, costs, mileage, photos, documents, hotspots, filters, 3D models and settings — is saved only on your device, inside the app. We have no copy of it and cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Camera and photos

If you allow it, MyServ uses the camera and your photo library only to attach photos and documents to your records. Photos are copied into the app on your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Backups and transfers

You can export a vehicle backup file, or send a vehicle to another nearby iPhone running MyServ. These only happen when you choose to, and go only where you send them (for example AirDrop, the Files app, or directly to a nearby phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi). Nearby transfers go straight between the two devices and are encrypted; they do not pass through any server. A backup contains everything saved for that vehicle, so please check it before sharing it with anyone.

Local network

MyServ asks for local network access only so that nearby phones running MyServ can find each other for transfers.

In-app purchases

Extra garage slots are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your payment details or Apple ID information. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase was made.

Network connections

The 3D viewer downloads its graphics library from a public content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net), and if you add a 3D model from a web link, the model is downloaded from that link. Like any web request, these services can see your device's IP address. No personal data from the app is sent with these requests.

Children

MyServ does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
