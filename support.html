How do I add another vehicle?

Tap your car's name at the top of the Overview tab to open your garage, then tap Add Another Vehicle. If your garage is full, tap Buy Another Slot.

I bought a slot — how do I get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Open the garage, tap Buy Another Slot, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

On the old phone go to More → Data Management and either Send to Nearby Phone (both phones open MyServ, close together) or Export Vehicle Backup and AirDrop / save the file. On the new phone accept the transfer or open the file, then tap Restore.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. MyServ has no account and no cloud copy, so make a backup before deleting the app or changing phones. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I use my own 3D model?

Yes — More → 3D Model lets you upload a .glb / .gltf file or paste a link. Models are saved inside the app so they keep working offline.
